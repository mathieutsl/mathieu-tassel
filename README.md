## 🚀 Current & Recent Projects

### 🏛️ CEPREMAP — Research Internship *(Summer 2026)*

Working under the supervision of [Sébastien Villemot](https://www.villemot.name/) and [Gauthier Vermandel](https://vermandel.fr/) on integrating a **social learning expectations mechanism** (Grimaud, Salle & Vermandel 2025) into [Dynare](https://www.dynare.org/).

The project extends a standard New Keynesian DSGE framework by replacing rational expectations with an adaptive, belief-updating mechanism — agents learn from past forecast errors through a social learning process rather than forming model-consistent expectations.

**Methodology:**
- Bayesian estimation of the model using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), with Metropolis-Hastings sampling to characterize the posterior distribution of structural parameters
- Model comparison between rational expectations and social learning specifications on euro area and US data
- Counterfactual analysis to assess how heterogeneous expectations dynamics contributed to post-pandemic inflation persistence and the subsequent rate hiking cycle
